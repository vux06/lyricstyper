♪lyricstyper

privacy policy

lyricstyper is a small personal project: a typing test where you type song lyrics in sync with the music.

what we collect

  • When you sign in with Google, we receive your Google account ID, name, email address and profile picture. We use them only to identify your account.
  • Your test history, personal bests, settings and recently played tracks, so they are available on any device you sign in from.
  • A session cookie that keeps you signed in for up to 30 days.

what we don't do

  • We don't sell or share your data, show ads or use third-party analytics.
  • We don't access your Gmail, Drive, contacts or any other Google data.

third parties

Song search and audio come from YouTube / SoundCloud through yt-dlp. Lyrics come from lrclib.net and lyrics.ovh. These services see only the song you search for, never your account.

deleting your data

Sign out and email the address shown on the Google sign-in screen to have your account and data deleted.
