a typing test made of song lyrics

lyricstyper is a free typing test where the text is the lyrics of a song you choose. Search any song on YouTube or SoundCloud (or paste a link) and the synced lyrics are found for you. The song plays and you type along, with the line being sung highlighted, so typing practice feels like singing along on your keyboard.

When the song ends you get your WPM, accuracy, raw WPM, consistency and a chart of your speed over the song, plus a personal best for every song. Sign in with Google to keep your typing history and bests on every device.

how it works

  1. search a song: type a title or artist, or paste a YouTube or SoundCloud link.
  2. type the lyrics as the music plays. The indicator shows whether you are behind or ahead of the singer.
  3. see your results: words per minute, accuracy, errors and how much of the song you typed.

features

  • lyrics in sync with the music, from lrclib.net, with the sung line highlighted
  • song: off mode for a silent lyrics typing test with no music
  • real typing stats: wpm, raw wpm, accuracy, consistency, missed and extra characters
  • personal bests and history for every song, with "play again"
  • forgiving options: ignore case and punctuation, simplify accents, drop ad-libs
  • 5 themes, adjustable playback speed (0.5×–1.25×) and lyrics offset

learn the song first

Don't know the words yet? Open the animated lyrics player to watch the song as a lyric video in karaoke, kinetic, typewriter, neon or flow style, then come back and type it.

faq

is lyricstyper free?

Yes. The typing test and the lyrics player are free. You only need a Google account to sign in.

how is wpm calculated?

Like other typing tests, one word is five characters. By default wpm uses your active typing time, so long instrumental breaks don't lower your score. You can switch to total time in settings.

can I type lyrics without the music?

Yes. Turn song: off to type the lyrics in silence, at your own pace.

what if the lyrics are wrong or out of sync?

Click change lyrics to pick another match, search lrclib yourself or paste your own lyrics (plain text or .lrc). Use lyrics offset to fix timing.

does it help me type faster?

Typing songs you love keeps practice fun, so you practice more. Real lyrics also bring in slang, contractions and punctuation that word-list tests skip.
