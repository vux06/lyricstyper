animated lyrics player turn any song into a lyric video, right in your browser

a lyric video generator for any song

lyricstyper listen plays any song from YouTube or SoundCloud as an animated lyric video. The synced lyrics are found automatically and move with the music on a blurred cover-art background with drifting colours, film grain and a beat pulse driven by the bass. No editing, no upload, no watermark: search a song and press play.

5 lyric styles

  • karaoke: each word fills with colour as it is sung, like a karaoke screen
  • kinetic: bold uppercase words pop in on the beat (kinetic typography)
  • typewriter: letters are typed out with a blinking cursor
  • neon: glowing words that flicker on
  • flow: a scrolling lyrics list. Click any line to jump to it

made for learning lyrics

Use it to learn the words of a song, to sing along or as a fullscreen lyrics screen. Change the font (7 choices), background, text size, speed and lyric offset, loop the song, and use the keyboard: space play/pause, ←/→ seek, ↑/↓ previous/next line, 1–5 lyric style, f fullscreen.

Know the words? Try the lyrics typing test and type the song while it plays.

faq

is the lyric video maker free?

Yes. Sign in with Google and play as many songs as you like.

where do the synced lyrics come from?

Time-synced lyrics come from lrclib.net, with lyrics.ovh as a fallback. You can pick another match or paste your own .lrc file.

can I make it fullscreen?

Yes. Press f or the ⛶ button. The controls hide while the song plays, so it works as a karaoke or lyrics display.

the lyrics are early or late. how do I fix it?

Open Aa options and change the lyrics offset until the words line up with the singer.
